INTEGRATION WITH PERM_CTRL (PERMISSION CONTROLLER)
================================================================================

The equal-status controller is designed as a companion to the permission-
certificate controller (perm_ctrl). Together they form a complete memory
boundary protection system:

  perm_ctrl (Permission)
    ├─ Certificate validation (SHA-256)
    ├─ Scope checking
    ├─ Integrity checking
    ├─ Epoch validation
    └─ Quota management

  equal_status (Parity/Non-Discrimination)
    ├─ Identity/origin/class discrimination prevention
    ├─ Decision caching (97%+ hit rate)
    ├─ Refusal consistency enforcement
    └─ Registry-driven perturbing set control

Integration: decision = (perm_decision AND epoch_decision) OR cached_decision

The equal_status controller is SMALLER (0.066 mm² vs 0.38 mm²) and LOWER
POWER (2 mW vs 14 mW) because it uses pure cache-based logic with no
cryptographic primitives.
